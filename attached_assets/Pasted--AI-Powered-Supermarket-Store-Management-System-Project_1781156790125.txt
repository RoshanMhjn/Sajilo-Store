# AI-Powered Supermarket Store Management System

## Project Overview

Build a modern, enterprise-grade, AI-powered Store Management System for supermarkets, retail stores, wholesalers, and multi-branch businesses.

The system should provide complete management of:

* Inventory
* Products
* Categories
* Suppliers
* Purchases
* Sales
* Billing/POS
* Staff
* Payroll/Stipends
* Customers
* Expenses
* Analytics
* AI-powered forecasting and business insights

The platform must be scalable, responsive, secure, and suitable for both small stores and large supermarkets.
